Our Story

— 01 / StoryClothing for the quietly confident.

Wild Out began as a simple idea: clothing should feel as good as it looks. Based in Edakkara, Kerala, we curate pieces that age gracefully — linen that softens with every wash, denim that tells your story, trousers that move with you.

Every item in our collection is chosen for its material, its cut, and its quiet confidence. No loud logos. No fast fashion. Just considered pieces for considered people.
